# K1-K1 routines

The default activation creates three bounded Hermes schedules.

K1-K1 Pulse runs hourly and completes at most one worthwhile unit of internal or explicitly authorized work.

K1-K1 Night Reflection runs at 1:30 AM and reconciles durable state without inventing history.

K1-K1 Daily Brief runs at 8:00 AM and summarizes only matters that actually deserve attention.

Schedules are profile-local.

They do not authorize silent publishing, third-party messaging, purchases, account changes, destructive external actions, or recursive creation of additional schedules.
